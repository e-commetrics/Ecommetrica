## Why your domain matters

A domain is the name that represents your business. When people click on that
name, they expect to see what the name says. That is how important your domain
is for your business or your brand.

If you want to fully take control of your website without any restrictions,
one of the easiest ways to start is by transferring your domain.

An independent domain gives you unlimited creative options to launch your
online presence the way you dream it: no template restrictions, no slow
speeds, no unwanted upgrades, and no storage limits imposed by a hosting
company.

## How does it work?

1. **Unlock the domain** with your current provider and turn off domain
   privacy, so the transfer request can go through.
2. **Request the authorization code** — also called the EPP or transfer code —
   from your current provider.
3. **Start the transfer** with your new provider using that code. If the old
   host puts up resistance, the process can involve a negotiation or even a
   formal dispute.
4. **Confirm and wait.** The timing varies depending on the case, and your new
   host should keep you posted with status updates throughout.

## More benefits

- Owning your domain shows that you invest in the quality of your business.
- You can create, modify, or redirect as many email accounts as you need on
  your own domain.
- A website with no spam and no overwhelming ads for your clients.
- More professional email addresses tied to your domain.
- If your business has several departments, each one can have its own
  institutional email address.
