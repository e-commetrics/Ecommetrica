Today, artificial intelligence can write, edit, and even run code without you
needing to be a developer. It's great for quick automations or short-term
fixes.

But when it comes to your brand identity and long-term scalability, AI falls
short.

## Today's shortcut is tomorrow's headache

Shortcuts save hours today, but they often produce rigid code, poor SEO, and a
generic design that looks just like your competitors'.

At Ecommetrica we think of every project as a system: diagnosis, definition,
architecture, and activation — not a sum of automatically generated pieces.
