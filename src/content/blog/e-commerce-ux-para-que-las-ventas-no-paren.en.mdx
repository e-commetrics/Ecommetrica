Here's everything you need to know if you're not sure about investing in your
business's website. And we're bringing you the pages that will inspire what
your user experience should look like.

We know your product is incredible — but how do you get that across through a
screen or a phone? Even more so when you're selling a snack, a drink, or a
service. If the page has no design and no experience, it sadly doesn't sell
anymore.

That's where e-commerce UX comes in: designing efficient, attractive interfaces
that create a memorable experience, so the customer spends more time on the
site, fills their cart, and recommends the brand.

## The e-commerce UX essentials

- **Operational simplicity.**
- **Design that complements the product or service**, making it stand out.
- **Information security.**
- **Logic in the page's navigation.**
- **Catalogs and menus** that are easy to understand and browse.
- **Product photography combined with UGC** or testimonials.
- **Simple checkout pages** that show progress.

One of the most common mistakes in e-commerce is dumping everything on the home
page. Nobody likes walking into a place and finding it all cluttered, or being
ambushed by an endless list of options they never asked for.

Below is our pick of pages with flawless UX — and what we like about each one.

## What we like about these pages

They name things the way their customers already name them — no extravagant or
technical terms — putting creativity first.

And that's a core part of e-commerce UX: if a shopper lands on the page for one
specific product, the promotions show up in strategic spots — bright colors,
different typography, or their own photo and promo page — so the shopper adds
more products to the cart.

## Four pages, four takeaways

From the four pages you just looked at, we can conclude that:

- All of them use icons to accentuate information.
- They show testimonials or ratings.
- They use photos of real people using the product, or explanatory videos.
- They make sure the *add to cart* shows up at the strategic moments.
